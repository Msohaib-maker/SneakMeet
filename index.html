The host is requesting wellness reports

      
The host would like to receive a short wellness summary from you during this meeting.

      
    
        
  • If you agree, this app measures your pulse, breathing and HRV, plus face signals (face in frame, facing the screen, talking, blinking, expression scores), on this laptop from your camera.
    • 
        
  • Your live readings (once per second) and a summary every ~25 seconds are sent only to the host, peer-to-peer. The host sees them next to your video; you and the others do not. No video frames are sent, and nothing is stored on a server.
    • 
        
  • The host's app may send these numbers to Google's Gemini AI about every 40 seconds to write a short brief, including an engagement estimate. Your name and video are never sent, only an anonymous label (like “P1”) and the numbers. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use this data to improve its products.
    • 
        
  • Everyone in the meeting sees a “Reporting to host” badge on your video while this is on.
    • 
        
  • You can stop at any time with the Report to host button. Declining does not affect your meeting.
    • 
        
  • These are wellness estimates, not a medical diagnosis.
